🏘 What is House Hacking?

House hacking means buying a multi-unit property, living in one unit, and renting out the others. As a Veteran, you can do this with $0 down using your VA loan benefit.

Example: Buy a 4-plex for $400K. Rent 3 units at $1,200/mo = $3,600/mo income. Your VA payment might be ~$2,300/mo — meaning tenants pay your mortgage and you build equity for free.
